SACRED ADORNMENTS

THE DANCER'S ATTIRE & ORNAMENTS

Every element of a Bharatanatyam dancer's attire carries spiritual significance and artistic purpose.

Consecrated Salangai (Ankle Bells)

Multi-tiered brass ghungroos blessed in the sacred Salangai Pooja before being worn for stage recitals.

Kanchipuram Silk Costumes

Handwoven silk tailored with dramatic accordion pleats that fan open in deep Aramandi postures.

Authentic Temple Kemp Jewelry

Head ornaments symbolizing the Sun and Moon (Surya-Chandra), waist belt (Oddiyanam), and jhumkas.

AESTHETIC EMOTION THEORY

THE NAVARASA EMOTIONAL SPECTRUM

Explore the nine primary emotional states from Natya Shastra that disciples bring to life through facial Abhinaya, Hastha Mudras, and Margam repertoire.

Rasa 01 • Devotion & Love शृङ्गार • भक्ति

SHRINGARA & BHAKTI

Divine Communion & Sacred Longing
Bhava Rati (Love)
Deity Sri Vishnu
Key Mudra Hamsasya & Pataka

The crown of all rasas. Depicts the individual soul's (Jivatma) sacred yearning to unite with the supreme divine (Paramatma), lyrical grace, and poetic romance in Jayadeva's Gita Govinda Ashtapadis.

Repertoire Focus Padams, Javalis & Ashtapadis
Rasa 02 • Wonder & Awe अद्भुत • विस्मय

ADBHUTA (WONDER)

Cosmic Mystery & Divine Awe
Bhava Vismaya (Awe)
Deity Lord Brahma
Key Mudra Alapadma & Mukula

Wide-eyed astonishment unveiling cosmic visions — such as Mother Yashoda gazing into infant Krishna's mouth and beholding the entire rotating cosmos, planets, and galaxies within.

Repertoire Focus Krishna Sancharis in Varnam
Rasa 03 • Heroism & Valor वीर • रौद्र

VIRA & RAUDRA

Heroic Valor & Righteous Wrath
Bhava Utsaha / Krodha
Deity Indra & Rudra
Key Mudra Dhanur Hastha & Shikhara

Heroic valor and sacred indignation against darkness. Portrayed through grounded warrior stances, bow-drawing mudras (Dhanur Hastha), and rhythmic stamping depicting Goddess Durga slaying Mahishasura.

Repertoire Focus Devi Stutis, Jatis & Teermanams
