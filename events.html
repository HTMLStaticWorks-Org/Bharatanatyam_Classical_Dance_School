THE MARGAM REPERTOIRE

THE TIMELESS CONCERT SUITE.

Every Bharatanatyam recital follows the structured Margam ("pathway") formalized by the legendary Thanjavur Quartet, taking the rasika on a transcendent journey:

  • Alarippu: Rhythmic invocation waking each limb with sacred poise
  • Jatiswaram: Pure sculptural dance synchronizing swara patterns
  • Shabdam: Introductory narrative mime invoking divine epics
  • Varnam: 45-minute summit uniting complex teermanams & Sancharis
  • Padams & Tillana: Soulful poetry culminating in celebratory joy

AESTHETIC ELEGANCE

KANCHIPURAM SILK & TRADITIONAL ORNAMENTS.

A Bharatanatyam dancer's attire reflects centuries of temple iconography. Disciples are adorned in handwoven Kanchipuram silk costumes tailored with accordion pleats that accentuate deep Aramandi postures, accompanied by sacred Salangai bells, natural red alta, and intricate Kemp ornaments.

Salangai Bells

Multi-tiered brass ghungroos producing crisp rhythmic acoustic resonance.

Sacred Alta

Auspicious red dye highlighting intricate hand mudras and foot strikes for the audience.
