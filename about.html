TRADITION & ETHOS

THE FIVE PILLARS OF OUR PARAMPARA

The sacred principles that govern every disciple's training at Nrityanjali Academy.

01

SHRADDHA

Deep devotion to Lord Nataraja and reverence for the sacred lineage.

02

SADHANA

Uncompromising daily physical practice to cultivate geometric purity.

03

LAYA

Absolute mastery of Tala cycles, internal tempo, and metric solkattu.

04

BHAVA

Subtle emotional authenticity awakening the nine classical Rasas.

05

SAMARPANAM

Surrendering artistic ego in dedication to the divine and audience.

FACULTY & MASTERS

EMINENT GURUS OF THE ACADEMY

Celebrated performers, scholars, and gurus dedicated to nurturing the next generation of soloists.

FOUNDER & ARTISTIC DIRECTOR

Guru Dr. Rukmini Rajagopalan

Direct disciple of legendary Kalakshetra maestros with over 45 years of performing and teaching experience. Has guided more than 180 disciples to acclaimed Arangetrams worldwide.

Specialty: Grand Margam Varnams & Natya Shastra Theory
NATTUVANGAM & TALA MAESTRO

Guru Sri V. Sundaresan

Renowned for his electrifying rhythmic articulation on bronze taalam cymbals and solkattu recitation. Directs the rhythm section for all academy Arangetrams and festival recitals.

Specialty: Teermanam Choreography & Complex Tala Cycles
SENIOR ABHINAYA GURU

Vidushi Meenakshi Iyer

Celebrated for her profound portrayal of Navarasas and delicate Jayadeva Ashtapadis. Guides senior disciples through nuanced character portrayals and Sanskrit lyrical interpretation.

Specialty: Padams, Javalis, Sanchari Bhava & Sanskrit Poetics
NRITTA & JATHI SPECIALIST

Vidushi Priyadarshini Rao

Leading soloist who conducts the Prarambhik and Madhyama foundation tracks. Specializes in geometric body alignment, high-speed footwork clarity, and dancer conditioning.

Specialty: 64 Adavu Series, Aramandi Stamina & Tillanas
LIVE MUSIC ACCOMPANIMENT

OUR RESIDENT CARNATIC ENSEMBLE.

True classical dance thrives only in dialogue with live classical music. Nrityanjali is proud to maintain its own full-time Carnatic chamber orchestra for student training and stage debuts:

● Vocal: Vidwan K. S. Ramanathan & Vidushi S. Gayathri
● Mridangam: Vidwan Tanjore R. Balasubramanian
● Violin & Flute: Vidushi Sharada Narayanan & Vidwan M. V. Swamy
