PROGRAM SPOTLIGHT

THE ARANGETRAM SOLOIST GRADUATION TRACK

The Arangetram (literally "ascending the stage") is the pinnacle moment of a classical dancer's discipleship. At Nrityanjali, we provide an all-inclusive 12-month mentorship framework that prepares the disciple physically, mentally, and musically for this monumental sacred debut.

THE 8-PIECE MARGAM REPERTOIRE:

  • 01. Pushpanjali & Alarippu: Invocation with flowers & rhythmic neck/eye balance
  • 02. Jatiswaram: Pure Nritta set to melodic swara combinations
  • 03. Shabdam: Devotional stutis with early narrative mime
  • 04. Varnam: The 45-minute masterpiece combining Nritta and Abhinaya
  • 05. Padams & Javalis: Deep emotional poetry and Sanchari improvisation
  • 06. Dhanashree Tillana: Fast rhythmic crescendo of joy and sculpturesque poses
  • 07. Mangalam: Sacred salutations to Lord Nataraja, Guru, and audience
