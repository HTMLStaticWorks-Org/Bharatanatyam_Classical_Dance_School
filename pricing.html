TUITION TRANSPARENCY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers regarding our enrollment process, tuition billing, make-up class policies, and student progression.

Can adult beginners with zero prior dance experience enroll?
Yes, absolutely. Our Level 1 Prarambhik batch welcomes students of all ages, including adult beginners. Gurus start with foundational principles — developing posture alignment in Aramandi, grounding the feet through basic Tatta and Natta Adavus, and introducing classical Hastha Mudras in a supportive, encouraging environment.
How does monthly billing work, and are there semester discounts?
Tuition is billed on the 1st of each calendar month via our secure Student Portal. Disciples paying for a full 6-month semester or 12-month academic year in advance receive an automatic 10% discount. Additionally, a 15% family rebate applies when two or more siblings or family members are actively enrolled.
What practice attire and footwork accessories are required?
For initial training, students wear comfortable cotton dance practice kurtas or salwar sets with a dupatta fastened firmly around the waist. Salangai (brass ankle bells) are not worn immediately — they are consecrated and bestowed during the auspicious Salangai Pooja ceremony once the Guru certifies the student's footwork stamina and adavu clarity.
What happens if a student misses a scheduled batch class?
Registered disciples can book up to two makeup classes each month through the Student Dashboard in parallel batch sessions. Furthermore, practice audio tracks, solkattu recitation recordings, and adavu breakdown notes are accessible 24/7 in your online student library.
How soon can a disciple prepare for their solo Arangetram debut?
The Arangetram is a formal, multi-hour solo stage debut demonstrating mastery of the full classical Margam (Alarippu through Tillana). Disciples typically qualify for the 12-month Arangetram Residency Track after 5 to 7 years of consistent training and formal evaluation by the artistic directors.
Can I switch my batch timings if my academic or work schedule changes?
Yes. Batch transfer requests can be submitted through the Student Dashboard at any time. Subject to capacity in the target batch and level compatibility, schedule changes take effect at the beginning of the next calendar week with no transfer fees.
